# Contributing to DPP

Contributions must improve the protocol through evidence, demonstrated operational need, or a clearly reproducible defect.

## Acceptable Contributions

- Contradictions or ambiguities with concrete examples
- Reproducible failures under real AI-assisted workflows
- Proposed wording that closes a demonstrated loophole
- Benchmark results comparing identical tasks with and without DPP
- Corrections to formatting, grammar, or cross-references

## Required Proposal Format

1. Identify the affected principle or section.
2. Describe the exact defect or operational problem.
3. Provide a reproducible example.
4. Propose complete replacement wording.
5. Explain compatibility effects.
6. State why the change improves DPP rather than merely reflecting preference.

Submissions containing placeholders, vague future work, or decorative restructuring may be closed without adoption. A constitution needs criticism, not furniture rearrangement.
