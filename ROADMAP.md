# DPP Roadmap

The roadmap records concrete work that advances DPP as a usable public standard. It is not a warehouse for attractive future nouns.

## Current Release: v1.3

Completed:

- 24 constitutional principles.
- Production Acceptance Criteria.
- Reader and Operational editions.
- Public licensing and attribution.
- Governance and semantic versioning rules.
- Quick-start adoption guidance.
- Reproducible benchmark protocol and submission template.
- Structured contribution and issue workflows.

## Next Evidence Milestone

The next milestone is not another principle by default. It is operational evidence.

Required work:

1. Publish at least three complete control-versus-DPP benchmark case studies.
2. Include at least one coding task, one research or analysis task, and one document-production task.
3. Preserve exact prompts, outputs, model metadata, scores, and critical-failure flags.
4. Identify repeated failure patterns that DPP prevents, reduces, or fails to address.
5. Propose revisions only where the benchmark evidence supports them.

## Candidate v1.3.x Work

Patch releases may include:

- clarifications that preserve existing obligations;
- formatting and navigation improvements;
- corrected cross-references;
- benchmark rubric clarification; and
- adoption examples that do not alter the standard.

## Candidate v1.4 Work

A v1.4 proposal requires evidence for at least one compatible normative improvement, such as:

- clearer severity treatment for critical failures;
- a formal vocabulary for fact, inference, estimate, and speculation;
- stronger rules for tool-use verification;
- explicit artifact-integrity requirements; or
- a conformance declaration format.

These are candidates, not commitments. They must earn adoption through demonstrated need.

## Longer-Term Direction

Subject to evidence and contributor capacity:

- platform-specific integration guides;
- machine-readable conformance metadata;
- independent benchmark replication;
- public release archives;
- a conformance test suite for AI-assisted software workflows; and
- translations that preserve normative meaning.

## Release Gate

No roadmap item is considered complete until the artifact exists, is linked from the repository where appropriate, has been inspected, and does not depend on fabricated evidence or missing implementation.
